This is the first post on *Latent Space*, a notebook where I'll be writing about AI, machine learning, and ideas at the edge of what we currently understand about both.

The aim isn't to chase the news cycle. It's to slow down and write carefully about things that are interesting — paper readings, small experiments, plots from notebooks, and the occasional opinion when I think I've earned one.

## Table of contents

## What I'll be writing about

A non-exhaustive list:

- **Paper notes** — what I learned, what I'm skeptical of, what's worth stealing
- **Experiments** — code, plots, results, and the things that didn't work
- **Concept primers** — explanations of ideas I had to explain to myself
- **Field notes** — thoughts on where ML practice is heading

## A few words on the name

In machine learning, **latent space** is the hidden, low-dimensional representation a model builds of the world. It's where similar things end up close together, where structure lives even when the surface looks chaotic.

That felt like the right metaphor for a blog — a space where ideas in different shapes can sit near each other and let me notice the connections.
